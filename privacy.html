Privacy policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Summary

Doorstep works on your phone. Your places, reminders and activity log are stored only on this device and are never sent to us. There is no account, no advertising and no analytics.

What the app uses

  • Location: to notice when you leave or arrive at places you saved. It is processed on your phone. Doorstep keeps a short log of departures and arrivals on your phone for troubleshooting; it never leaves the device.
  • Notifications: to show your reminders.
  • Bluetooth (Android, only if you create a Bluetooth reminder): to notice when a paired device such as your car connects or disconnects.
  • Health (iPhone, only if you link a reminder to a medication): to read the medications you choose to share from the Health app, and whether today's dose is logged, so Doorstep doesn't remind you of a dose you already took. iOS asks you for each medication. Doorstep never writes to Health. What it reads stays on your phone and is never sent to us; a linked reminder keeps only which medication it is for.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple or Google. Doorstep only asks the store, on your phone, whether you have Pro. We never receive your payment details.

Backups

On iPhone, Doorstep's data is left out of backups, in iCloud and on your computer, so restoring a backup to a new iPhone does not bring back your places and reminders. On Android, your Google account backup may include Doorstep's data, depending on your backup settings.

Your choices

You can turn off location, notifications or Bluetooth for Doorstep at any time in your phone's settings. On iPhone, you choose which medications it can read in Settings > Health > Data Access & Devices > Doorstep. Deleting the app deletes all of its data.
